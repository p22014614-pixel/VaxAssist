Vaccines help protect you and your community

Use this simple guide to learn what vaccines do, plan questions for your healthcare provider, and prepare for routine or travel vaccination.

Book an appointment Getting started

Getting started

Vaccination needs vary by age, health history, previous doses and destination. A clinic can help you check what is recommended for you.

Routine vaccines

Keep a record of vaccines you have received and ask a healthcare professional whether any doses are due or need updating.

For children

Bring your child's vaccination record to appointments. Your local health clinic can explain the schedule and answer questions about each dose.

Before travel

Share your destination and travel dates with a clinic early. Some travel vaccines may need to be given before your trip.

What to ask at your appointment

Which vaccines are right for me?

Ask about recommendations for your age, medical conditions, previous doses and upcoming travel.

What should I expect?

Ask about common side effects, how to manage them, and when to contact the clinic after vaccination.

What should I bring?

Bring your vaccination record and a list of medicines, allergies or questions you want to discuss.

Personal advice matters. This page is general information and does not provide an individual vaccination schedule or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional. Recommendations can vary by location and personal health history.
